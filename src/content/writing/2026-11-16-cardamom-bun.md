---
title: "An Infatuation"
date: 2026-11-16
categories: ["Orange Peel"]
---

<p>Gracefully desperate, I walked into the fourth bakery in search of a cardamom bun, wishing I had never tasted a cardamom bun.<br>

<p><br>

<p>My life before eating it was just as full, many other activities just as delightful. Now no day goes by without my craving, for something that could have gone on existing without my knowing.<br>

<p><br>

<p>The city is an island of the Sirens, filled with seductive sense objects that are no good for me. The more I might love it, the more reluctant I am to try it. For life is not about accumulating all things I love, but rather, loving the un-de-loverable.<br>

<p><br>

<p>Four weeks and six cardamom buns later, desire glided just below the threshold of reason, when an Enlightenment sort of courage compelled me to sever my tie with the cardamom bun.<br>

<p><br>

<p>A whole week, I did not search for a cardamom bun.<br>

<p><br>

<p>That Saturday, I hopped on the subway downtown at 6am, gracefully desperate, to try Mary O’s Irish soda bread.<br>
