<p>For two years now, I have tucked my gaze near. I have focused on building (repairing) a relationship with myself, and building myself. The former endeavor centered around meditation for the mind/heart, martial arts, choreography, yoga, acupuncture, diet, and sleep for the body. The latter centered around religious experiences and inquiries for the soul, the research praxis for the mind, and muscle mass building for the body. I entered a committed relationship, not just with myself but also with another person, which permeated all aforementioned spheres.<br>

<p><br>

<p>I am ready to lift my gaze, again.<br>
