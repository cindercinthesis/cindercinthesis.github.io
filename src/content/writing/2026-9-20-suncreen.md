<p>In Tibet, we used a lot of sunscreen.<br>

<p><br>

<p>“Your sunscreen smells bad.”<br>

<p><br>

<p>“No Dad, it’s the smell of black rice, it’s the selling point.”<br>

<p><br>

<p>Since then, every morning I put on sunscreen, I hear Dad’s voice. A feature, wronged as a bug, becoming a new feature featuring a voice and a memory so dear to me–it makes me laugh still, in my NYC apartment, two months later, 8000 miles away.<br>

<p><br>

<p>In this way, objects join the long procession of life. Everything is drawn in, and everything is let out.<br>
