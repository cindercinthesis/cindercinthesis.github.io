<p><strong>I</strong><br>

<p>Soon enough, you will forget<br>
<p>All the shots you missed; and did not miss<br>
<p>That the pool table existed<br>
<p>That you were feeling a fleeting moment of balance<br>
<p>Like a feather, free falling, drifting<br>
<p>Before it touches the ground<br>
<p>More tumbling to go<br>

<p><strong>II</strong><br>

<p>Here, everything is nothing<br>
<p>Packed into a shipping box labeled humanity<br>
<p>A nondescript sense of self-importance<br>
<p>Where desires and non-desires alike<br>
<p>Folding onto one another, a lasagna sizzling<br>
<p>With peace, in between the layers<br>
